Research

My research centers on continual learning: enabling AI systems to learn from non-stationary streams of data, tasks, and modalities while retaining previously acquired knowledge. My work spans computer vision and vision-language models, alongside research on world models and model-based reinforcement learning.

Continual Learning Multimodal Models World Models Reinforcement Learning

Research Themes

Continual Learning

I study continual learning for models trained on non-stationary data streams, where new classes, tasks, modalities, or distributions arrive over time. The goal is to learn new knowledge while mitigating catastrophic forgetting under memory and compute constraints.

  • Class-incremental and exemplar-free learning
  • Prototype-based representation consolidation
  • Knowledge retention, forward transfer, and replay
Related Work
ECA · ICML 2026 YoooP · TMLR 2025 Hybrid Memory Replay · arXiv

World Models & Reinforcement Learning

I study predictive models of environment dynamics and policy learning for planning and control. The goal is to improve dynamics prediction across robotic systems and learn policies that guide efficient model-based planning.

  • Knowledge-encoded trajectory world models
  • Action-chunk policies for online planning
  • Planner-guided policy learning
Related Work
WestWorld · ICML 2026 Spotlight SWAN · Manuscript

Continual Alignment for LLMs / MLLMs

I study continual adaptation of pretrained MLLMs and activation steering for LLM alignment. The goal is to adapt cross-modal alignment under sequential distribution shifts and steer language-model behavior while preserving pretrained capabilities.

  • Continual alignment for pretrained MLLMs
  • Capability preservation under distribution shift
  • Activation steering and preference alignment
Related Work
ECA · ICML 2026 ODESteer · ICLR 2026

Current Research

Ongoing · 2026–Present

Continual Reinforcement Learning for Humanoid Control

I investigate how humanoid robots can build on prior motion knowledge to learn successive tasks through online interaction while retaining performance on previously learned tasks. My current focus is on skill transfer, sample-efficient adaptation, and mitigating catastrophic forgetting.
